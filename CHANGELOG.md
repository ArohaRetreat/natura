# Changelog

## Natura 0.3.0 - 2026-10-31

- Expanded nature motion from four to twelve independently drawn studies with family filters, full playback controls and static reduced-motion states.
- Rebuilt weather as layered retreat landscapes with canopy/grass response, rain impacts/runoff, depth-aware mist, daylight and night lighting.
- Preserved the observed night palette across rain, mist, breeze and cloud conditions.
- Reworked component previews into a native interaction workbench with selection, press, disclosure and focus feedback plus implementation notes.
- Added a complete generated motion catalogue and transparent SVG exports.


## Natura 0.2.0 - 2026-10-31

- Added the atmosphere atlas: expressive serif/calligraphic roles, independent season/weather previews and distinct sun, rain, mist, breeze and night studies.
- Documented the fixed Hrudhi Farms location, proposed local editorial calendar, validated weather contract, freshness, severe-weather/static fallbacks and production provider gate.
- Adopted app-launchpad-template v1.9.0 in the official public ArohaRetreat/natura repository, preserving prior history and third-party notices.
- Added Aroha-only custom-source/asset terms and retained independent MIT/OFL permissions.


## Natura 0.1.0 - 2026-10-31

- Named the design system Natura and moved the canonical public app to Firebase Hosting at `/design/`.
- Added two botanical illustrations with original PNG and optimized WebP downloads; story sections can include either artwork in the website export.
- Replaced generic motion demos with separate leaf drift, seed germination, water ripple and trail journey SVG timelines.
- Added pause/resume, scrub, speed, reset, keyboard controls and a final static state for reduced motion.
- Added current field and motion guides, retained identity standards, corrected service icon documents and consolidated current asset packages.
- Kept white primary surfaces, transparent logos/icons and locally licensed bilingual typography.
- Preserved bounded browser-local drafts and standalone website exports; no Analytics or backend initialized.
