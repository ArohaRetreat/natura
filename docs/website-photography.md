# Website photography production

Ten selected owner photographs were edited with Adobe Photoshop/Lightroom tools before resuming the site build. Originals remain untouched. Twenty-megapixel PNG masters, Adobe-exported web compositions and SHA-256 provenance remain outside this public repository in the sibling Photo-Production directory. Only edited WebP derivatives ship; format encoding does not replace the Adobe edits.

Daylight: auto tone, Color - Natural, highlights -35, darks +20, saturation -7, vibrance +8, contrast +5. Night: original geometry, highlights -45, darks +25, exposure +0.15, saturation -3. Aggressive night auto-tone and incorrect automatic cottage leveling were rejected. Night remains night; clipped lamp cores are not reconstructed by highlight adjustment.

All ten masters and web exports were visually inspected. A palm extraction was produced but its pale fringe was not accepted; it is excluded. Desktop Photoshop became unresponsive opening that layer, so no manual edge refinement or layered PSD completion is claimed.

[Adobe review board](https://firefly.adobe.com/boards/id/urn:aaid:sc:AP:d7044243-3ce7-4a27-aaa0-4cb7c69c58aa) contains the ten edited photographs. Missing assets include correctly matched room interiors, a real entrance gate and an accurate route map. Botanical/architectural details are not proof of a particular room; replace them with labelled photographs through the studio when available.

## Complete source set, revision 0.5

All 35 photographs are now included. The remaining 25 full-resolution originals were uploaded through Adobe's block transfer and edited with its Photoshop adjustment API. Day: highlights -30, darks +14, contrast +6, saturation -6, vibrance +5. Night: exposure +0.2, highlights -45, darks +25, shadows +10, saturation -4. Hall interior: exposure +0.25, highlights -25, darks +18, shadows +12, saturation -4. Adobe normalized EXIF orientation in its PNG outputs; exported WebP compositions were reviewed in contact sheets. Motion-blurred photographs remain archive moments rather than primary architectural views.

Twenty-one HDR10 source films were reviewed at four timestamps each and converted to silent, fast-start SDR BT.709 H.264 web editions with a maximum dimension of 1280 px and 24fps. All have WebP posters. The real raised-room interiors are visible in these films; they are not assigned to unrelated cottage styles. The kitchen, library and pool are located from the owner's description and have contextual, accurately captioned photographs until dedicated views are supplied.

The complete public-safe source/output provenance is [website-media-manifest.json](website-media-manifest.json). New edited masters and private Adobe URLs are outside the repository in Documents/Codex/Aroha-Media-Production. Original files under Downloads/Aroha are untouched.
