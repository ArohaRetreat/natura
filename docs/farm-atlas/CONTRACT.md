# Natura farm atlas v0.1.0

Purpose: a browsable, reusable design library for Aroha's farm and nature website. The supplied context is 9,800 trees across 94 varieties. These are owner-supplied counts, not a connected inventory or independently audited census. No 94-item species list is invented. Animal artwork is a design proposal, not a claim that every illustrated animal lives at the retreat.

The atlas defines 96 distinct 24-unit line icons in five groups: 16 domestic animals, 20 tools, 20 tree/orchard structures, 20 biodiversity/weather subjects and 20 farm infrastructure items. Use one consistent 1.75-unit regular stroke or 2.25-unit strong stroke, rounded caps/joins, generous negative space and transparent canvas. UI icons serve navigation, service details, care information and farm maps. Pair unfamiliar or safety-relevant symbols with text; icon shape does not replace an instruction.

Sixteen editable colour animal illustrations use a 360x240 transparent artboard, named tail/head/ear/leg parts and bounded one-pass study timelines. Larger painterly animal references are a separate visual treatment. Bull, cow and water buffalo use distinct anatomy: bull shoulder hump/muscular neck, cow's lighter dairy silhouette and udder, buffalo's broad dark body and lateral swept horns. Dog designs are general domestic types, not veterinary breed identification. No animal biology or ownership is inferred from a photograph.

Exports: regular/strong SVG, transparent 128/256 PNG line icons; SVG symbol sprite and JSON catalogue; colour animal SVG/PNG; explicit native web motion studies and one-pass animated WebP previews; illustration PNG/WebP references. Every catalogue record has a stable lowercase ID, group, label, search tags and appropriate-use text. No opaque backdrop is embedded in icon/animal exports. A semantic rectangle, such as a crate or barn, is subject geometry rather than a canvas background.

The website shows searchable specimens, labels, view sizes, weights, an animal motion workbench, individually downloadable files and a full ZIP. Motion waits for Play, respects OS reduced motion, pauses on hidden tabs and disposes on route exit. Animal actions move named anatomy; a cat's blink/stretch, dog's tail/ears, bird's peck and ruminant's chew/tail do not use a shared whole-image bounce. No audio, livestock-management backend, tree census database or AI identification is connected.

Runtime contract v1: catalogue IDs are allowlisted, colours are currentColor or validated six-digit hex, sizes are finite and bounded 16-512, weights are regular/strong, labels are escaped. Species is a design-category string, not user-supplied markup. Export URLs come from known catalogue records. All search filtering is local and does not upload visitor text. The supplied farm context contains only numeric counts, attribution and inventory status; future species records require an approved name, stable taxonomic mapping, provenance and review before publication.

Custom art/code are restricted to authorised Aroha Retreat websites under the root licence. Keep font and MIT notices for third-party material. Generated painterly references and authored vector masters are identified separately. Future additions must be recognisable at 24px, checked on white/dark surfaces, exported with alpha and reviewed against the existing geometry. Do not multiply generic leaf silhouettes and label them as actual tree species.
